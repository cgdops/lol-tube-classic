# League Classic Off-Meta Wishlist

Here is a curated unlock order for champions that feature absolutely crazy, fun, off-meta builds in League of Legends Classic (similar to the battle-mage AP Soraka). 

## ~~1. Master Yi~~ (Owned)
* **Role:** AP Mid
* **The Build:** Deathfire Grasp (DFG) -> Rabadon's Deathcap -> Lich Bane
* **Why it's OP/Fun:** The legendary AP Master Yi. His Alpha Strike (Q) had a 1.0 AP ratio and reset on kills/assists with his Highlander (R). He could instantly wipe an entire team with Q resets. His Meditate (W) had a 4.0 AP ratio, meaning you could tank all 5 enemies and a turret simultaneously while healing to full health.

## ~~2. Sion~~ (Owned)
* **Role:** AP Mid / Top
* **The Build:** Mobility Boots -> Deathfire Grasp -> Rabadon's -> Lich Bane
* **Why it's OP/Fun:** Classic Sion had a 0.9 AP ratio on his Cryptic Gaze (point-and-click stun) and a 0.9 AP ratio on his Death's Caress (shield explosion). You walk up, stun them, pop your shield, and they instantly disappear. Un-dodgeable burst damage.

## ~~3. Warwick~~ (Owned)
* **Role:** AP Top ("Magewick")
* **The Build:** Sorcerer's Shoes -> Hextech Gunblade / Will of the Ancients -> Spirit Visage -> Rabadon's
* **Why it's OP/Fun:** Very similar to AP Soraka. Magewick's Hungering Strike (Q) dealt high percentage max health magic damage with a massive 1.0 AP ratio, healing him for 100% of the damage dealt. You are an unkillable drain-tank lane bully who slowly bites the enemy top laner to death while staying at full HP.

## 4. Nunu & Willump
* **Role:** AP Mid / Top
* **The Build:** Rod of Ages -> Rabadon's -> Void Staff
* **Why it's OP/Fun:** Ice Blast (E) had a 1.0 AP ratio and a devastating slow, making him an oppressive poke champion. The real fun was Absolute Zero (R), which had a jaw-dropping 2.5 AP ratio. Sitting in a brush and charging R would instantly one-shot entire teams if they walked in blindly ("Empire" combo).

## 5. Shaco
* **Role:** AP Top 
* **The Build:** DFG -> Rabadon's -> Void Staff
* **Why it's OP/Fun:** Pure psychological warfare (like Teemo). You stack Jack-in-the-Boxes (W) in top lane brushes. If the enemy top laner or jungler walks in, they get chain-feared and melted instantly by 0.2 AP ratios per box attack. Your Two-Shiv Poison (E) also had a 1.0 AP ratio for point-and-click poke.

## ~~6. Malzahar~~ (Owned)
* **Role:** AD Jungle
* **The Build:** Wriggle's Lantern -> Manamune -> Black Cleaver -> Bloodthirster
* **Why it's OP/Fun:** Classic Malzahar's Voidlings scaled with his bonus AD and armor penetration. You summon multiple Voidlings, and they literally maul the enemy to death while you suppress them with your ultimate. AD Malz could solo the Dragon at level 2 or 3 in classic LoL!

## ~~7. Gangplank~~ (Owned)
* **Role:** Crit / Avarice Blade Top ("Bankplank")
* **The Build:** Avarice Blades (stack them!) -> Infinity Edge -> Phantom Dancer
* **Why it's OP/Fun:** Before his barrel rework, GP's Parrrley (Q) was a simple point-and-click nuke that could critically strike and applied on-hit effects. In Classic, you could stack GP5 (gold per 5 seconds) items like Avarice Blade, farm safely, and then run around one-shotting squishies with a single Q.
