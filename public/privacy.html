LoL Tube Classic — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026
✓ Strict Zero-Data Retention & Zero-Sale Guarantee

LoL Tube Classic ("we", "our", or "the application") is an independent non-commercial web application dedicated to archiving classic 2009–2013 League of Legends YouTube videos. We are committed to upholding the highest privacy standards for all visitors.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, capture, or ask for any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, account credentials, or payment details. No user account registration or login is required to use this site.

2. Zero Data Retention & Storage

We do not store, keep, record, or retain any user activity, browsing patterns, IP addresses, search queries, or device metadata on any server or database. Search queries are processed in real-time within your browser solely to communicate with YouTube API endpoints and are discarded immediately.

3. Zero Data Sale or Commercial Sharing

We do not sell, rent, license, trade, share, or monetize user data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, tracking companies, analytics networks, or marketing partners under any circumstances.

4. Local Browser Session Caching

To optimize performance and minimize API quota usage, search query results may be stored temporarily in your web browser's local session storage (sessionStorage). This cached data remains exclusively on your own personal device and is automatically erased as soon as you close your browser tab or window.

5. Third-Party Embedded Services (YouTube)

This application uses YouTube Data API v3 and official YouTube embedded iframe video players to display public video content. By interacting with video players on this site, your interaction with YouTube is governed by YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Contact & Open Source Transparency

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may inspect the open source code repository or reach out via project maintainers.
